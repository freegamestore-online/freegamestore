About FreeGameStore

A platform of free, open-source browser games that respect your privacy. 90+ games and growing.

Our Philosophy

Free means free. Not freemium. Not free-with-ads. Not free-until-we-get-acquired. Every game on this platform is genuinely, permanently free to play. There is no monetization strategy. There is no plan to "convert" you into a paying customer.

We believe that classic games and creative new ones should simply exist for people to play, without friction, without commerce, without compromise.

Free means free

No freemium tricks. No ads. No "plans." These games will never ask you for money. If it's on FreeGameStore, it's yours to play forever at no cost.

Real games, not demos

Every game is a Progressive Web App. Install it on your home screen, play offline, get a native app experience. No app store required.

Works everywhere

Built for phones first, then scaled up to tablets and desktops. Responsive, fast, and touch-friendly on every device.

Private by default

No accounts. No tracking. No analytics. No cookies. Your data stays on your device. We can't see what you do because we never look.

How the games work

Each game on FreeGameStore is a standalone Progressive Web App (PWA) hosted on its own subdomain. When you visit a game, it loads in your browser like any website — but it can do much more:

  • Install to home screen — Tap "Add to Home Screen" and the game gets its own icon, launches without browser chrome, and feels native.
  • Works offline — Service workers cache the game so it works without an internet connection.
  • Saves your progress locally — High scores, settings, and game state are stored in your browser's local storage, on your device, under your control.
  • Updates automatically — When you're online, the game silently updates to the latest version.

The two platforms share DNA. You can always switch between the free and pro version of any game. If you're happy with free, stay there forever. If you want advanced features, Pro is one click away. No pressure, no dark patterns, no lock-in.

Creator Program

FreeGameStore is built by creators. Anyone can build a game for the platform. You get your own subdomain, auto-deploy on every push, and your game listed in the store for players worldwide.

The easiest way in: the free AI game builder (VibeCode) at console.freegamestore.online — describe your game, sign in with GitHub, and the AI writes and deploys it for you, free (on the built-in GitHub Models tier). Bring your own AI key for stronger models, or build locally with the CLI and your own AI coding tool.

Interested? Visit the Build page to learn more.
